Article: Parameterized Resampling Time-FrequencyTransform.
Digital Object Identifier: 10.1109/TSP.2022.3220027.  
Abstract: Many signals contain multiple components with time-varying instantaneous frequencies (IFs) sharing a common trend, such as mechanical vibration signals, speech signals, and biomedical signals. To analyze these types of signals and achieve higher time-frequency resolution, this paper proposes a new time-frequency analysis method—the Parameterized Resampled Time-Frequency Transform (PRTF transform). This paper adopts the concept of Generalized Parameterized Time-Frequency Transform (GPTF transform), using parameterized kernel functions to represent the reconstruction function, and further constructs time-varying and time-invariant reconstruction operators to eliminate mid-band variations and reposition the mid-band location. These operators can simultaneously improve the energy concentration of multiple components in the time-frequency representation (TFR). For different types of signals, typical kernel functions containing polynomial functions and Fourier series are given. A corresponding kernel estimation method is proposed, utilizing multiple components to detect the shared trend of IFs, and recursively approximates the kernel parameters. Numerical simulations and practical experiments have demonstrated the effectiveness of this method in improving the time-frequency resolution of non-stationary multicomponent signals.
